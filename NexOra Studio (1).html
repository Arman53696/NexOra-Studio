Astravolt

Fight enemy spacecraft and powerful bosses in futuristic arcade space battles.

Astravolt is an exciting space shooter game that takes players into futuristic space battles against dangerous enemy spacecraft and powerful bosses. Players can explore challenging missions, use different weapon systems, upgrade their spacecraft, and fight their way through the galaxy.

With engaging combat mechanics, boss battles, weapon upgrades, and achievement progression, Astravolt aims to deliver an immersive arcade space-combat experience.

My Smart Note

Keep notes, shopping lists, study plans, routines and reminders organized in one place.

My Smart Note is a modern productivity application designed to help users organize their everyday lives. Whether it's personal notes, shopping lists, study plans, routines, or important reminders, the app provides a convenient digital space to keep information organized.

Featuring a modern interface and a focus on usability, My Smart Note aims to make everyday note-taking and personal organization simpler and more efficient.
